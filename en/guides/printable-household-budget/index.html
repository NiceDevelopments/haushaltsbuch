How it works

  1. Fill in the first page once: what comes in each month and what goes out for sure. At the bottom you see what's left to live on.
  2. Every month, copy that number to the top of a month sheet.
  3. Write down what you spend. At the end of the month, add it up and take it off.

If one sheet isn't enough, just print another.

Questions

Does the template cost anything?

No. Print it, fill it in, done.

Do I have to start in January?

No. Start in whichever month you like.

What if one sheet isn't enough?

Print a second month sheet and carry on there.
