How much for …? Rules of thumb and calculations

“Is that a lot?” is a question that comes up as soon as the first expenses are written down. These guides give rules of thumb for rent, savings and an emergency fund, show how to find your own grocery number, and how to work out a daily budget from your own numbers.

Rules of thumb are a starting point, not a verdict – only your own spending shows what fits you.

  • How much can I spend per day?Your daily budget in three steps – at the start of the month and halfway through.
  • How much should I spend on groceries a month?Averages for 1 to 4 people – and your own number in four weeks.
  • How much rent can I afford?The rules of thumb, their limits and a check with your own numbers.
  • How much should I save each month?Rules of thumb, the right order and a sum for your own amount.
  • Emergency fund: how much, how to build it, where to keep itWork out the size from your own spending and build it step by step.
